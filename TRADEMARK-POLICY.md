# VCCC Trademark Policy

This document outlines the policy for using the trademarks associated with the Viral Concurrent Cellular Collectives (VCCC) protocol. 

While the VCCC protocol and its associated documentation are freely available under the MIT License, the trademarks, service marks, and logos associated with VCCC are strictly protected. This ensures that when people see the VCCC name, they know it refers to the authentic principles and community established by Daniel Worth, preventing confusion, fraud, or misrepresentation.

## The Trademarks
This policy covers the following protected marks:
*   **Viral Concurrent Cellular Collectives™**
*   **VCCC™**
*   Any associated logos or visual branding (present or future)

## Acceptable Use (What You Can Do)
You may use the VCCC marks without prior written permission in the following contexts:

*   **Accurate Reference:** You may use the name to accurately refer to the original VCCC protocol or white paper (e.g., "This group operates using principles from the VCCC protocol").
*   **Compatibility:** You may state that your own independent software, scripts, or organizational tools are compatible with the protocol (e.g., "An RSS parser designed for VCCC feeds").
*   **Community Discussion:** You may use the name in articles, blog posts, tutorials, and academic discussions about the protocol.

**Requirement:** In all acceptable use cases, it must be completely clear that your project is independent and not officially sponsored by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the creator of VCCC.

## Unacceptable Use (What You Cannot Do)
You may NOT do any of the following without explicit prior written permission:

*   **Naming Your Project or Software:** You may not use VCCC or Viral Concurrent Cellular Collectives in the name of your own commercial software product, business, or formal organization (e.g., you cannot name your app "VCCC Tracker" or your company "VCCC Solutions").
*   **Domains and Usernames:** You may not register domain names, social media handles, or GitHub organizations that include the trademarks. 
*   **Implied Endorsement:** You may not use the marks in any way that suggests your project, cell, or product is officially maintained, endorsed, or sponsored by the VCCC protocol's creator.
*   **Merchandise:** You may not sell merchandise (shirts, stickers, mugs, etc.) bearing the VCCC name or logo.

## Proper Attribution
When using the marks in an acceptable context, please include the trademark symbol (™) on the first and most prominent appearance, and include the following attribution statement in your documentation or footer:

> *"Viral Concurrent Cellular Collectives and VCCC are trademarks of Daniel Worth."*
