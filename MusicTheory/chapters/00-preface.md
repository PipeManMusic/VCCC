# Preface {-}

Welcome to the **VCCC Jazz Study Book**. This project is an open-source music theory workbook presenting lead sheets, chord voicings, guitar tablature, and harmonic analysis of essential jazz forms and progressions.

All musical examples are rendered dynamically from LilyPond score sources.
