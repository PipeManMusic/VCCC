## Theoretical Analysis

### 1. The 'A' Section (I - VI - ii - V)
The primary progression establishes tonic stability in Bb using the standard turnaround (**Bbmaj7** – **G7** – **Cm7** – **F7**). The chromatic passing chord **Bdim** in measure 3 acts as an ascending root substitution connecting **Bbmaj7** to **Cm7**. In measure 6, the motion targets **Eb7** ($IV^7$), borrowing the blues subdominant inflection before chromatically passing through **Edim** back to the turnaround.

### 2. The 'B' Section (The Ragtime / Dominant Cycle)
The bridge abandons the tonic key and executes an 8-measure cycle of fifths/fourths: **D7** ($III^7$) $\to$ **G7** ($VI^7$) $\to$ **C7** ($II^7$) $\to$ **F7** ($V^7$). Each dominant chord lasts two measures, building forward harmonic motion that resolves naturally back to the tonic Bb on the final 'A' section recap.

### 3. Guitar Voice Leading
In the 5th-to-8th fret box, keep voice-leading minimal. Notice how the 3rd of each secondary dominant steps down a half step to become the 7th of the next chord across the bridge cycle (e.g., $F\sharp$ in **D7** steps down to $F\natural$ in **G7**).
