# After You've Gone

![](scores/after-youve-gone.ly)

## Theoretical Analysis

### 1. Non-Tonic Opening (IV to iv)
Unusually for a standard in C major, the tune opens on the subdominant **Fmaj7** before moving to **Fm6** ($\text{iv}^6$). This minor plagal motion introduces the expressive flattened sixth scale degree ($A\flat$), which resolves down by half-step to the fifth ($G$) of the tonic C major chord.

### 2. Secondary Dominant Chain
In mm. 4–6, **A7** acts as $V^7/\text{ii}$, resolving to **D7** ($V^7/V$), which in turn feeds the primary dominant **G7**. This cycle of fourths drives momentum back to the tonic **Cmaj7** in measure 7.

### 3. Passing Diminished Motion
In measure 13, **D#dim** connects **Dm7** to **E7** (or C/E). Its guide tone $F\sharp$ functions as a sharp fourth chromatic passing bass, pulling voice leading smoothly into the secondary dominant **E7**.
